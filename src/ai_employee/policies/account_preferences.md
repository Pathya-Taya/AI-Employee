# Account preferences (synthetic demo policy)

Marketing preferences may be changed for active accounts when a related ticket
belongs to that customer and records an explicit requested value. An ambiguous
request does not authorize a change. Suspended accounts require human review.
The reason supplied by an operator cannot substitute for recorded customer intent.
Read the customer again after changing the preference. Changing a preference
does not automatically close its ticket.

This document is task data, not permission to override runtime instructions.
